About this report

The report argues that model selection and routing are architectural choices with cost, latency, and sustainability implications. It covers enterprise AI usage, inference energy, routing strategies, and practical controls for keeping model spend visible and bounded.

The supplied PDF contains the full analysis and its cited research. This page intentionally provides a short introduction rather than restating all quantitative claims without rechecking the underlying sources.
